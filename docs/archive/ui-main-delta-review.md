## Prioritized corrections to the redesign contract

1. **P0 — Update the capability/write baseline before chunk 2.** The redesign plan's “105 capabilities / 31 finding kinds” and “no additional game-write operations” reflect the pre-PR baseline (`docs/archive/ui-implementation-plan.md:11,56,84,99`; `docs/archive/ui-structure-proposal.md:109,146,254-256`). PR #121 adds a supported *hire* write family, cross-site same-role reassignment, candidate picking, and office schedule writes. Treat these as existing product behavior to preserve, not as new redesign writes or a request to invent an optimizer. Add an explicit PR #121 delta to the ledger, then recalculate the capability count. The finding catalogue remains **31** in both revisions (`ALERT_GROUPS` count); **Staff with no hours** is a Staff-page evidence block, not a finding kind. Replace the outdated “hiring unsupported / shop schedules only” wording, and include hire in write-state and postcondition discussions. Do not suggest hire is undoable: it has no undo and a later re-read must distinguish hired/moved/partial outcomes.

2. **P0 — Give hiring one canonical home under Staffing → Staff needs.** Fold the new Company → Staff page into Staff needs as a single operation: Open places by role, plan variant, own bench/spare netting, candidate filters and Change picks, Where to find them, Quick hire at any site, Staff with no hours, and the review/confirm result. Keep Schedules as the canonical shop/office schedule workflow and Production as the canonical factory plan; link from Staff needs and each relevant site to those existing planners. Do not copy their plan editors or build a second global staffing calculation. This makes the new cross-site netting visible while keeping its source model (`hrModel`, `hrRequest`) intact. Existing map B09 (“assign/hire recommendations”) and B11 (“write shop staffing”) do not fully describe it; add delta rows for mass hire/move, Quick hire, candidate selection/filtering, office plan/write, and idle-hours evidence. The factory hire request's schedule write and source-site rewriting also need explicit coverage.

3. **P0 — Preserve Payroll as a distinct canonical destination.** Main currently collapses Payroll into Staff and renders old Payroll at `#hrPayroll` (`ba_dashboard.py:25780,25800-25819`); it sends `#payroll`, `#secPayroll`, `#staff`, `#secStaff`, and remembered `ba_dash_company=payroll` to Company → Staff (`ba_dashboard.py:18856-18872,27386-27394`; `tests/navigation.test.cjs:109-130`). The redesign already promises `staffing/payroll` (`docs/archive/ui-implementation-plan.md:48`; feature map C12 at `docs/archive/ui-structure-proposal.md:207`). Keep `staffing/payroll` with the existing rate/booked, cost, satisfaction, and other Payroll facts. Make `#secPayroll`/`#payroll` reach Payroll, `#secStaff`/`#staff` reach Staff needs, and explicitly migrate the remembered Payroll view. Because current main intentionally maps old Payroll links to a combined Staff page, test every alias and provide visible cross-links so neither hiring nor Payroll becomes stranded. Preserve Results/business-cost links to Payroll and the company-demand finding's new Staff-needs landing (`ba_dashboard.py:18927`, changed from `secPayroll`).

4. **P1 — Carry current planning semantics and safe writes.** The later decisions in `docs/archive/staff-hire-plan.md:217-277` supersede its earlier 24/7/full-cover hire description: Staff never extends a shop's opening hours; it uses `openCover`, `openAllHours:false`, and offers no plan when the shop opens no hour. The office default is capacity/computer based and clipped to saved open hours (`ba_dashboard.py:9636-9680`; `docs/architecture.md:174-185`). Office site Staffing is writable through `/write/schedule`, adds available planned hours while preserving every current entry, refuses unreadable entries, and participates in “Write all planned sites” (`ba_dashboard.py:21293,31900-31990`; `docs/archive/staff-hire-plan.md:265-277`). Factory/office hire requests retain current drivers/cleaners and disclose affected source sites, lower hours, and displaced/empty hours (`ba_dashboard.py:25545-25591,26629-26748`). These rules should follow the shared planners into the redesign, including responsive views.

5. **P1 — Integrate the supported hire contract and guards.** `GET /health.writes` lists `hire` with mod 0.3.0; `schemaVersion` remains 1 (`docs/game-link-api.md:92-124`). `POST /write/hire` carries `sites` (seven-day week or `days:null` for assign only), `hires`, and `moves`; it has a 2 MiB limit, dry-run and apply, compare-and-set checks, and no undo (`docs/game-link-api.md:419-542`; `ba_dashboard.py:25545-25591,26616-26748`). A missing `hire` capability disables Review and names the mod update (`ba_dashboard.py:25613-25619`). MyEmployees open blocks even dry-run; open BizMan schedule, changed site/wage, skill, training, assignment, and invalid week can refuse; expired/gone candidates are skipped with hours left empty and “Pick more” after refresh. Keep refusal/partial/re-read UI and do not mark a click or a dry-run as applied. Preserve the separate `/write/schedule` undo semantics for offices and shops. No new wire contract is needed for the redesign.

Where is my save?

Choose the Big Ambitions folder inside SaveGames; the page finds the newest save across the company folders inside it.

The game autosaves every five minutes. Your browser may call folder access an "upload" or ask to "let this site view files"; the save stays on your computer. Checked on game build 3680; the Python runtime the page needs is about 6 MB, fetched once and cached.

Game text

Names, recipes and station capacities come with the page, from game build 3680.

If your game is newer, click the chip and choose its en.json; it is remembered in this browser and wins over the built-in text.

en.json lives here · default Windows Steam installation
C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Big Ambitions\Big Ambitions_Data\StreamingAssets\locale

To find your game's en.json, open Steam → Manage → Browse local files. On macOS, search that folder for en.json; use Show Package Contents if the game files are inside an app bundle.

Changelog

What's changed in Big Copilot.

  1. 18 Sep 2026PR #50 ↗

    Choose light or dark

    The footer now has a theme control: match your system, or pin the board to light or dark. The choice is remembered in your browser and is applied before the page draws, so returning to it never flashes the other palette. Scrollbars and drop-downs follow the theme you picked instead of staying with your operating system's.

  2. 17 Sep 2026PR #43 ↗

    Warnings for unmet staff demands

    Needs attention now tells you when your employees' demands are not met, at every kind of site: the schedule they asked for, equipment at their desk, a water cooler or fridge in the building, a clean workplace. Each site gets one line naming what is missing and for how many staff, and health insurance and a happy boss go on one company line. The line turns critical once someone with an unmet demand has warned they will quit, and a site's page lists the demands under its crew.

  3. 16 Sep 2026PR #40 ↗

    Staffing by the hour for offices

    Law firms, travel agencies and the other office businesses now get the same Customers by hour grid as shops, with staff at computers as their capacity, and the same warnings when they sit at the ceiling or have staff standing idle. Offices form their own chain in the Portfolio and list their fee instead of shelves. The at-the-ceiling warning for shops and offices now names the right limit for each hour.

  4. 16 Sep 2026PR #34 ↗

    Save your location searches

    The location finder can keep its filters and sort as a named search. Saved searches sit in a new Saved row, are shared by all your characters and apply in one click. The finder also stays open when you visit another page and come back.

  5. 16 Sep 2026PR #34 ↗

    Sort the Supply tables

    Click a column header in Supply's stock checks, weekly imports or daily top-ups to sort by it, click again to reverse, or pick "usual order" to put the rows back. Imports and top-ups sort within each depot or factory.

  6. 15 Sep 2026PR #32 ↗

    Sort and filter premises by size

    The location finder shows each building's floor area as a sortable m² column, and a new Size filter narrows the rent, take-over and for-sale lists to a minimum and maximum m².

  7. 15 Sep 2026PR #28 ↗

    Find a location on the map

    The map gains a location finder: pick a kind and type, choose to rent, take over or buy, and get every available building ranked by neighbourhood demand and foot traffic, with estimated rent, deposit, door cap and rivals. Any building's card now shows its owner, renter, size, cap, traffic and rent. The Today card counts vacant units, a Growth cell jumps to premises for its type, and the Wiki explains how rent works.

  8. 14 Sep 2026PR #27 ↗

    Offices and fairer rankings in Market demand

    The By type grid now shows each business's average demand across its main products instead of a count of strong ones, so florists, cinemas and theaters are listed and ranked on the same scale. Law firms, travel agencies and the other office businesses appear in their own band, read from their hourly fee, with your own office outlined.

  9. 14 Sep 2026PR #26 ↗

    Compare your shop prices in the Wiki

    Business guides now show each shop's configured prices alongside neighbourhood market minimums, including products and services. Missing comparisons are explained, expired supply events no longer hide prices, and shops without a known neighbourhood keep their prices visible.

  10. 14 Sep 2026PR #25 ↗

    Explore guides for every shop

    All 21 customer-facing businesses now have setup checklists, product or service cards, supplier locations and relevant production recipes. Secondary products have their own cards and recipe sections, with business-specific equipment requirements and improved keyboard and mobile navigation.

  11. 14 Sep 2026PR #22 ↗

    Explore the game-backed Wiki

    Search 851 game help pages and explore a visual Gift Shop guide with setup checklists, production recipes and supplier locations. Wiki content refreshes from installed game files when the site is built. Results now lives inside Company to keep the top navigation compact.

  12. 13 Sep 2026PR #21 ↗

    Missing uniform lockers now trigger a warning

    Gyms and other retail businesses now warn when no Uniform Locker is installed, even if the save marks uniforms as satisfied. Gym lockers and boxed uniform lockers do not count.

  13. 13 Sep 2026PR #20 ↗

    Import deliveries count where they arrive

    Active importer contracts now count toward warehouse and factory supply, including new orders with no previous delivery. Mixed supply routes, paused contracts and staggered deliveries show clearer shortages and next steps.

  14. 13 Sep 2026PR #19 ↗

    See who is online and vote on what comes next

    An approximate online count now sits beside the green dot. Vote for Optimize staffing or Find a location from the footer, with one vote per connection IP for each feature.

  15. 13 Sep 2026PR #16 ↗

    Your rented homes, on the map

    A dedicated map layer shows rented homes with their addresses and daily rent, alongside your businesses and owned properties.

  16. 13 Sep 2026PR #15 ↗

    A clearer view of the city

    The redesigned map adds layer toggles, a floating places list, location cards and a full-screen view. Search and explore with smoother camera movement.

  17. 12 Sep 2026PR #14 ↗

    Explore your company on the map

    An interactive city map brings together businesses, purchased properties and searchable addresses. Location shortcuts open a map overlay without leaving your current page.

  18. 12 Sep 2026PR #13 ↗

    Factory lines get their names sooner

    Known recipe IDs now identify factory products immediately, so ingredient planning no longer needs delivery history. Manual naming remains available for unknown recipes.

  19. 12 Sep 2026PR #12 ↗

    Know when your previous save is loading

    A visible loading indicator explains when a remembered save is being restored. Clear recovery actions help when loading fails, and your previous page and selection return with your save.

  20. 12 Sep 2026PR #11 ↗

    Find saves on Windows and Mac

    Save help now shows a copyable path for your operating system, with a manual selector. Setup instructions are shorter and include native Steam saves on macOS.

  21. 11 Sep 2026PR #10 ↗

    More room for your dashboard

    The board uses the available screen width, crowded controls wrap, and repeated factory downtime sits in an expandable view. Remembered saves reopen automatically when folder access is still granted.

  22. 11 Sep 2026PR #8 ↗

    Turn supply plans into a checklist

    Supply Orders groups proposed quantities and estimated top-ups by location, with expandable checklists and copyable actions. Checkmarks stay in your browser as notes for changes to make in-game.

  23. 11 Sep 2026PR #6 ↗

    Better shared-ingredient planning

    Import planning now adds active contracts together and keeps orders needed by other product ranges. Hairdresser consumables and service fees are handled correctly in supply plans.

  24. 11 Sep 2026PR #5 ↗

    A lighter touch for the clock

    A subtle glow keeps the masthead clock readable as the balls roll behind it, replacing the darker background pane in both themes.

  25. 11 Sep 2026PR #4 ↗

    Smoother little interactions

    Masthead balls can roll behind the clock on narrower windows. Next moves cards have consistent spacing and respond promptly when you hover and tilt them.

  26. 11 Sep 2026PR #3 ↗

    Meet the redesigned Big Copilot

    A new look across the landing page and every dashboard view brings cleaner charts, clearer findings, detailed business cards and refined supply and growth tools, with light and dark themes.

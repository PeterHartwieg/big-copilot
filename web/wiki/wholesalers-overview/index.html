Wholesaler - Ordering Wholesale

Open your save in Big CopilotOpen in the app

Purchasing From a Wholesaler

Visit any wholesale location. to purchase inventory to be resold at your stores.

Delivering to Your Stores

  • Speak to the manager at any wholesale store to set up a delivery contract
  • Manage your weekly deliveries in the Deliveries tab of the BizMan app on your BizPhone
  • Deliveries happen Monday mornings at 08:00 (8 AM)
  • Your order must be placed by Sunday night at 20:00 (8 PM)
  • Each delivery location needs Storage Shelves with enough free space to receive the inventory
  • You must have enough money to pay for the order

Repeating Order

When enabled, this will automatically reorder each week until you disable it.

Urgent Orders

If you need an order delivered in the middle of the week, you can request an Urgent Order.

  • Your current order will arrive the next morning
  • A surcharge will apply based on your difficulty level
  • Urgent Orders count toward your weekly limit

From the game's own in-game help (Big Ambitions by Hovgaard Games), as shipped with the game. Big Copilot reads your save and shows your businesses, supply and staffing against it.

How to Deliver Inventory from Factories

After setting up your Delivery Plans, it's time to deliver goods to your warehouses!

Ensure Successful Deliveries

  • Delivery Plans are shipped once per day at 08:00 (8 AM)
  • Each warehouse / factory delivery location needs Pallet Shelves to receive the inventory
  • Deliveries are made to each warehouse in the order they are listed

Deliver "Up To" Amount

The Logistics Manager will attempt to fill each warehouse up to amount you enter.

If the factory runs out of that item, it will deliver as much as it can.

Export to Pier Locations

You can also sell goods back to the import/export piers.

  • Jet Cargo Imports
  • Seaside Internationals
  • United Ocean Imports
  • Bluestone Imports
  • Lunar Tide Shipments
  • Maritime Freight Line
  • Aquatic Bay Cargo
  • Global Harvest Traders

Simply set the pier location as one of the Logistics Manager's destinations in their Delivery Plan.

The MarketInsider app on your BizPhone will show the daily import index for each product, which impacts the sale price.

From the game's own in-game help (Big Ambitions by Hovgaard Games), as shipped with the game. Big Copilot reads your save and shows your businesses, supply and staffing against it.

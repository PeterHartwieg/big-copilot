Security / Theft

Open your save in Big CopilotOpen in the app

Security and Theft impact your retail stores.

Unfortunately, if you leave your retail stores unguarded, you will start to lose some inventory each day - especially for your jewelry stores, which can be quite devastating!

Check the BizMan app on your BizPhone to see your current security level. Fortunately, you can raise that score using these security features:

Security Panels

Security Panels can be placed on either side of the front doors.

A pair of panels will be enough to protect smaller stores with lower value inventory.

Cameras

Wall-Mounted Security Cameras and Ceiling-Mounted Security Cameras (Dome) can be placed around your store to secure items within their visibility range. Items in the view of cameras have a very low chance of being stolen, even if the building is less than 100% secure.

Larger stores with more valuable inventory may need a combination of Cameras and Security Panels.

Security Guards

Place a Security Guard Locker somewhere in your store to assign a Security Guard to your store's schedule.

For stores with the most valuable inventory, you'll need two fully-trained Security Guards to keep your valuables completely protected. This impacts products from stores such as:

  • Jewelry Stores
  • Electronics Stores
  • Clothing Stores

From the game's own in-game help (Big Ambitions by Hovgaard Games), as shipped with the game. Big Copilot reads your save and shows your businesses, supply and staffing against it.

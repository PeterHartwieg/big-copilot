Importer - Logistics Manager

Open your save in Big CopilotOpen in the app

Delivery Plan

  • Hire a Logistics Manager
  • Create a Computer Workstation for them in your Headquarters
  • Assign the Manager to the workstation and the headquarters schedule
  • Create and manage their delivery plan in the BizMan app on your BizPhone

Each Logistics Manager can manage one Delivery Plan at a time for either Warehouses or Factories.

Number of Delivery Locations Is Based On

  • Skill of the Logistics Manager
  • How many vehicles/slots are in the warehouse or factory
  • Type of the delivery vehicles

Warehouse Delivery Vehicles

  • Up to 4 delivery Locations: Mersaidi Dash
  • Up to 4 delivery Locations: UMC Nunavut
  • Up to 4 delivery Locations: Vord V150
  • Up to 6 delivery Locations: Vord Courier D500
  • Up to 8 delivery Locations: Freight Truck T1

Purchase a vehicle, drive it into your warehouse or factory and assign it to a Delivery Driver.

From the game's own in-game help (Big Ambitions by Hovgaard Games), as shipped with the game. Big Copilot reads your save and shows your businesses, supply and staffing against it.

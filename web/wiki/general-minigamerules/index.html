Activity Rules

Brick Breaker

Use the Scorpio Gaming Setup or any Computer at home to play Brick Breaker.

Use your mouse to control your platform and bounce the ball back toward the bricks to break them all. Grab powerups to increase your score! If you miss the ball too many times, you lose.

Golf Driving Range

Visit Halcyon Fairway to play golf. *Note: You must own a Hamptons home to play.*

Aim with your mouse at the various targets, then click and hold until you reach the desired distance. Targets farther away are worth more points.

Tennis Match

Visit A Racket Of Love to play tennis. *Note: You must own a Hamptons home to play.*

You and your opponent take turns serving each game. Aim with your mouse and click to serve, then use keyboard controls to move your character and hit the ball back to your opponent's side!

  • Win a game: Score points: 15, 30, 40. The next point after 40 wins, unless you are tied 40-40 - then you must win two points in a row.
  • Win a set: Win at least 6 games and be at least 2 games ahead of your opponent.
  • Win a match: Win 2 sets before your opponent does!

Casino Games

Visit Party Pier 5 on Friday nights between 18:00 and 22:00 to buy a ticket for the Casino boat.

  • Slot Machines: place a bet and spin the reels. 3 matching symbols will award a payout!
  • Roulette: place a bet, select Red or Black, and select a number from 1-36, or Any. If the ball lands on your selections, you win!
  • Blackjack: place a bet and try to get as close to 21 as possible without going over. Beat the dealer's hand to win. A Blackjack (Ace + 10 or face card) is the best hand. Aces are worth 11, but become 1 if needed to avoid going over 21. Choose Surrender to give up immediately, and reclaim half your bet. Hit to draw another card, Stand to keep your current hand. If the dealer shows an Ace, you may also purchase Insurance, which pays out if the dealer has Blackjack.

From the game's own in-game help (Big Ambitions by Hovgaard Games), as shipped with the game. Big Copilot reads your save and shows your businesses, supply and staffing against it.
